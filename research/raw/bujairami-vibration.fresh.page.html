Freshness, elegance and really good vibes
Vibration immediately won me over from the very first spray. The opening is fresh, citrusy and pleasantly fruity at the same time. In particular, the combination of bergamot and grapefruit gives the fragrance a sparkling and lively presence right away, without coming across as too aggressive or artificial.
After a few minutes, Vibration becomes noticeably more interesting to me. The initial freshness gains more depth through ginger and the slightly floral notes, while the fragrance as a whole becomes increasingly softer and more elegant. I particularly like this transition.
In the drydown, the warmer and woodier facets come to the fore more strongly. Amber, musk and cedar give the fragrance a beautiful foundation and prevent it from being just another fresh summer scent.
For me, Vibration works both for everyday wear and for somewhat dressier occasions. Fresh enough for warm days, yet elegant enough that you can easily wear it in the evening as well.
