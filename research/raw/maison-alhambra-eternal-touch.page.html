Cozy, Boozy and warm
This Fragrance is a nice alternative to anyone looking for the TF TV Alternative. Before there was a bottle called Tobacco Touch, I think this is exactly the same juice as before. It isn't a complete 1:1 this one is a bit more boozy, Clove spiced and sweet. Also has a lot spice, mostly cinnamon and cloves. If you like Gourmands this one is something for you. It opens very sweet and boozy. It later develops into a cinnamon cake, with the sweet tobacco in the background. It then fades into a Tobacco Vanilla scent. It projects amazingly well, lasts 6-10h with strong projection. At the beginning, in the first 3h is really strong. I dont recommend spraying more than 3-4 times. It does indeed make some people uncomfortable, this because of the sweetness it has. So don't overspray ! In terms of compliments I have had a lot, and although it is sweet it leaves a good trail and I had people come closer to me just to smell it. Specially ladies, they love a scent like this. I payed 15€ for this and it was an amazing price taking in consideration what it has to offer. Again it isn't a 1:1 to TF it is more harsh and all over the place sweet, specially in the opening, very boozy and Clove spicy. Only after 1-2h in, does it starts to smell like TV. Again the blend isn't the same as a High End perfume buuut for this price I can say its a Winter/Night Banger.
Overall 7.8/10

Cheap TF TV clone
This is literally just Tobacco Touch in a new bottle, I heard Tom Ford started sending out cease and desists to a few companies due to the bottle designs and names.

It's an ok cheap TV clone, with at best, mild performance. It's a little rough at first like many Arabian frags from this time period, things do improve in the dry down. I do appreciate that they got some of those dried fruit notes in there that some other clones skipped out on.

The one issue I have here is that the user ratings are very deceptive: this lasts 5-6 hours at best with with heavy application. Projection is good for the first hour, maybe 90 minutes. I'm not going to complain too much because it is dirt cheap.

My bottle was the old style that looked like a TF and had the old TT name. The presentation was good for that one, it came in a double layered nice quality box and the bottle had nice atomizer, which is always nice. The bottle did have issues with leaking from the top.

Side rant: Amber Oud Tobacco Edition is superior to this in every single measurable attribute besides price per ml. Just get that instead, it lasts over 12 hours on skin and days on clothing, and while it can also be a little hectic upon application from a fresh bottle, it smooths out very nicely in the dry down. The opening of AO TE also gets very smooth after 4-6 weeks with some air in the bottle and it only costs a little more than Eternal Touch/Tobacco Touch.

Composition of Great Spice
I don't know the "original," but this one really stands out! Especially when it comes to the spice. Here, juniper berries roll over cloves, coriander leaves, finely crumbled, coriander, and allspice - a whole shelf of various spices. All of this is intertwined with aromatic tobacco leaves for fine cigars or pipes.
Despite all the many "edible" elements, it is not a gourmand scent for my nose. Although cocoa and vanilla are also present. The former is bittersweet, while the vanilla is dark and smoky. Only the tonka bean adds something very subtly "gourmandish."
The specified dried fruits are so tightly woven with the bitter-dark woods that they hardly make an appearance. Especially the clove becomes particularly prominent later, but it certainly does not overshadow the other spicy components. These create a strong chorus around the clove opera diva. And all the other fragrance notes serve as a successful backdrop for her grand entrance.
A perfume for everyone who likes it heavily spiced, including gingerbread and spice bread lovers, of course ;-))

Christmas in a Spray
Eternal Touch (Maison Alhambra) smells to me like a liquid gingerbread house: warm, spicy, and distinctly sweet with a clear vanilla and spice note in the foreground. The combination of tobacco, spices, and the sweet base strongly evokes winter baking vibes.
The fragrance is overall very rounded and cozy, but clearly geared towards cold days and not really suitable for everyday wear in summer. For me, it's a true feel-good scent with a strong Christmas character.

Not just spice when cooking
This scent is hard to beat for this price; when you spray it, you immediately smell the spice that this fragrance brings. This spice is enjoyable, reminiscent of an oriental kitchen, not too much. For some, it might be too much, but I find it just right, as this strong spicy scent doesn't last long and transitions into a super soft, very delicious vanilla note that is still accompanied by the spice, which only takes a backseat. When you walk by, people can smell you; everyone can smell you, so it is very strong - about 6-7 sprays and you're good to go. Best on cold days, as it can be quite intense in summer, but this fragrance is really enjoyable. Compliments are guaranteed, and the price-performance ratio is truly world-class.
