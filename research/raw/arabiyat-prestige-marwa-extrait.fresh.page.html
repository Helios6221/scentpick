Another dupe bites the dust…
Every fragrance influencer has got this before it reached retail . So you’re only going to find positive things about this online..

However, here is my real world review .

There was a bloke on TikTok saying that this was better than imagination which I thought was a massive call. Nonetheless I purchased a bottle for AU$79 and it arrived in the mail a couple of days later.

Not to my surprise, this is absolutely nothing like imagination. This is thick and this is extremely resinous. Imagination is light and airy. This is thick and heavy.

The sparkle for imagination to me is that lightness in the air and that bright watery refreshing sparkle this loses that all.
Almost to a point where this has a bit of incense in it.
Make no mistake this is 100% in Arabic twist on imagination.

Don’t let these influences fool you. It is their job to do exactly that. Nothing will come close to imagination. There is a reason why imagination is still pumping out thousands of sales a day. If these dupes were half as good as what they say they are that would’ve slowed down a long time ago.

Would I recommend this for someone who’s looking for imagination DNA that can’t afford it to be honest with you, no I wouldn’t because you’re going to be chasing the OG no matter how many dupes you have you’re going to want to scratch that itch put away a little bit of money every week until you can afford the real thing even if you can get a little vial that’s better than this .

The only thing that this has got going for it is longevity which in 2026 some people would rather smell terrible and have longevity then have a scent that is quality.

But TikTok told me it was good ?
If you’re looking at Marwa Extrait thinking it’s a dupe or clone of Louis Vuitton Imagination, do not even think about it. I am completely baffled by the influencers and reviewers claiming these two smell close.
The Scent Profile Reality Check
What Imagination Is: A masterclass in transparency clean, airy, sparkling citrus, tea, and a weightless breeze.
What Marwa Extrait Actually Is: It goes in a completely different universe. It is an incense-heavy floral with a dense, smoky edge that entirely obliterates any sense of freshness.
The Blind Test Reaction
To make matters weirder, I had two people with zero fragrance knowledge smell it. Without skipping a beat, they both looked at me and asked: "Is this a women's perfume?" That heavy, floral-incense character is miles away from the crisp, fresh, versatile DNA of Imagination.
Final Verdict
If you want an airy, soapy-clean citrus scent, Marwa Extrait is a total miss. Save your money and don't fall for the TikTok hype.

Smoother than the EDP
This is the Marwa I wanted. The DNA is still there: the soapiness, tea and freshness, but everything feels smoother, softer and much better blended. The spice is less prominent, while the other elements feel more integrated.

In the drydown, the difference is even more noticeable. After smelling the Extrait, the EDP almost smells like expired spices in comparison.

Scent: 8.5/10
Longevity: 8/10 (Lasts all day, but it becomes a skin scent after 2 hours)
Sillage: 6/10 (It doesn't really travel through the air as the EDP does)
