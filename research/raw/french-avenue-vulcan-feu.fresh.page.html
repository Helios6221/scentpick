Vulcan Feu
On the opening, the first thing I notice is a citrusy, sour mango note. It might come off as a bit unpleasant to some, but I personally enjoy it. As it dries down, the fragrance becomes more "mature" and I can clearly pick up cedarwood, ambergris, and tonka bean.

The opening is quite harsh, which is why I recommend going very light on sprays VERY LIGHT. As it settles, it becomes much smoother and more enjoyable.

I can also pick up a vanilla-ish feeling which is probably the tonka bean but I like it, creamy dry down compared to the harsh opening

God Of Fire but cheaper and better!
I’ve recently sampled God Of Fire just last week and though it smelled amazing, never had a mango/tropical focus fragrance in my collection.

The problem was it was way too expensive for what it offered, especially the performance. Instead I found out Vulcan Feu because it was hyped up. For around $50 USD, you can get an 3.38oz instead of 1.7oz for $250+ for God Of Fire. Once arrived on my doorstep, I’ve been wearing it everyday.

The smelled is about 90% similar but it’s slightly more woody than the OG. The performance is surprisingly better! I get a good 8-9 hours with moderate-strong projection with 5 sprays on skin. I had people around me saying I smell like a juicy ripped mango within 4 hours.

In my opinion, everything about this fragrance is pretty much better than what god of fire offered, get it now if you can since the price is drastically going up because of demand.

Atomic, Sour, Synthetic...
I blind bought this looking for a refreshing, fruity summer scent. OOPS. The blind purchase was a poor decision, and frankly an addictive relapse (I do however also partly blame the fragrancesphere hype machine). I sprayed twice on my chest without thinking (I do this with my softer fragrances often) and it gave me a pounding headache and made me question a lot of decisions in my life. For me, this smells like an incredibly synthetic, sour, and cheap mango note that is insanely strong, stays a completely unwelcome amount of time, no development, no nuance, like somebody yelling at the top of their lungs on a crowded train. I am now pondering how best to dispose of this bottle. I wanted to leave it downstairs at my apartment building anonymously but I realized I don't want to smell this on anyone within 50 yards of me ever again.
