Forbidden is the Fruit...The Dry Down is the Masterpiece
Aromatix Forbidden Fruit – 9/10
I've been really impressed with this release. It opens with a vibrant burst of blood orange and bergamot, backed by just enough cardamom to give it some warmth. As it settles, the red apple and peach keep the fruity character alive instead of letting it disappear after the opening. That's one of my favorite things about this fragrance—the fruit carries all the way into the drydown.
The drydown is where Forbidden Fruit really shines. The fruit gradually blends with warm amber, creamy vanilla absolute, benzoin, smooth sandalwood, cedarwood, and musk, creating a rich, polished finish that's sweet without becoming overly sugary. It evolves naturally and smells well put together from start to finish.
Overall, I'd give it a 9/10. If you enjoy modern fruity fragrances with a warm amber-vanilla woody drydown and appreciate a scent that evolves smoothly instead of changing abruptly, Forbidden Fruit is definitely worth trying.

A Fragrance that is Born to standout
So I got my hands on this today, and I can already say this new release is beter and higher quality than his previous ones. Not that the others were bad they were great , but this one is simply better. This is a first wear, so I’ll definitely update my thought after more testing and after it has some time to mature.

The fragrance is absolutely beautiful. If Platine Blanc is the fun, playful daily fragrance, then this one is more of a night-time, special-occasion scent. It’s truly unique. It opens with a beautiful note of apple and blood orange. I don’t get much of the cardamom at the start, it will appear more in the dry-down but there is definitely since i sense the fragrances giving those dark, slightly spicy vibe, with woody nuance After a while, the floral notes start to shine while the apple settles down, and the vanilla adds a sweetness — but not an overly sweetness like you might expect from a vanilla no more the elegant calming one .

Performance? So far, it’s good. I’m past the 6-hour mark; it’s no longer projecting strongly, but it’s still there, softly.

Sillage? Right from the opening it pushes hard, but after 10–15 minutes it calms down. It’s not a beast-mode projector, as I mentioned earlier — it’s more of a classy, elegant style of fragrance.

Overall, it’s a wonderful release, easily up there with Platine Blanc, and I feel I’ll enjoy it even more after letting it sit for a month. I’ll also re-test it when the weather gets cooler, because it’s really hot here at the moment. The batch is very recent (10/25), so some maturing will likely improve the performance and bring out certain notes even more.

8.75/10 — Great job, Neeb.

Cocktail on the Beach
It doesn't come from nowhere that the bottle design reminds me of a cocktail. To me, it smells like a summer cocktail. Sweet from the syrup, fruity, and yet somehow refreshing. It makes you crave more or was it the sea ;).

I have to say that Aromatix has really achieved something wonderful here, in my opinion. Despite its sweetness, it is not overwhelming. I think it is very pleasing and still has something very unique that I haven't smelled before. Unfortunately, I am not familiar with its supposed inspiration, so I approach this completely untainted. I can somewhat understand why some might get a children's bubblegum vibe, but I find that the fruitiness outweighs the chemistry. It gives me a cozy feeling, and I think of a sunset on the beach with a cocktail in hand and my toes buried deep in the sand. However, I don't think it's necessarily only suitable for summer. I believe it fits from spring to autumn and is definitely unisex.

Longevity is very good. Still below beast level but potent enough to last comfortably through the day. The sillage is pleasant and consistently radiates a good arm's length, and I don't feel that this diminishes over time. In my opinion, it shouldn't be stronger either, to avoid being intrusive.

Conclusion: A really successful fragrance, in my opinion, that gives me positive emotions. If I could, I would give it an 8.8. Thumbs up.
