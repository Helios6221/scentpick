Here's To Something To Look Forward To.
There is something a bit optimistic and hopeful about fragrances like this.

They evoke a summer which everyone is looking forward to in Europe and especially here in Dubai. The war will hopefully be over, the Middle East will be rebuilding trust and infrastructure, tourists will be flocking back in their droves again and the gloom and worry of the last few weeks will hopefully be a distant memory. The weather here this weekend has been cloudy, rainy, a bit chilly and a bit uncertain, mirroring the geopolitical situation and my own state of mind. So when I came across one of the most summery fragrances imaginable and spent a couple of minutes trying the tester in the shop yesterday, it hit all the right notes.

Aquatica by Rayhaan evokes warm, balmy summer Caribbean nights to me. I've been to the British Virgin Islands and Aquatica is supposed to be a dupe of Virgin Islands Water by Creed. I can say the feel is definitely authentic, both the original and this bold reimagining by Rayhaan: Rum cocktails with lime and coconut. Crystal clear turquoise sea. Sensual and warm skin scents. Sun cream and tanning oils.

You have to be a bit careful of the term "dupe" I think. Some dupes are shameless, artless, rather cynical moneygrab copies, whilst the other end of the scale are artistic inspirations that are similar to (but deliberately not the same as) their fragrance muses. Aquatica is a very high quality production that sits a bit more toward the latter end of the scale, for me. It is clearly based on VIW, but is stronger, lasts longer and is a bit more aggressive in the opening. There is more lime in there than the original and the coconut is deeper and more biscuity, almost like that smell you get from lime segments stuffed in the necks of cold Corona beer bottles. If you know, you know.

It settles very nicely but diverges a bit more from the VIW DNA as it does so. I actually prefer it.

It isn't as sweet as the original I don't think. I always found VIW to be sweet and tender but incredibly short-lived and a bit limited in its appeal because of this. Aquatica is not a 1 to 1 copy. It's around 80% the same. Aquatica is not as refined, it smells a little more synthetic in the opening. The lime is a tiny bit screechy and harsh to begin with and the blending is nowhere near as good, but it is stronger, it does still smell great and it definitely does perform better. My bottle is a Feb 26 production and hasn't had time to mature, and it still performs better than VIW. The overall effect is akin to if Creed ever did a slightly less sweet Elixir concentration of VIW, if that makes sense.

I owned and enjoyed VIW a few years ago but finished the bottle and never replaced it because I found the performance to be more style over substance. In fairness, I've almost never wished I had it to hand since it went and I didn't really miss it. It is expensive and opportunities to wear it living in rainy, cold Rotterdam were few and far between indeed. But since then I've moved back to Dubai and there are a lot more opportunities to wear a fragrance like this here. It can get as hot as 50 or even 52 degrees here in the Summer months and you want something fresh and summery for then that does perform in the heat but doesn't go sticky or cloying. Aquatica at US$25 a bottle also represents vastly better value, it must be acknowledged.

I (very deliberately) have very, very few dupes in my 100+ bottle collection; literally only this, Lattafa Khamrah Qawah and Obsidian which is also by Rayhaan. But in this case I think Aquatica earns a spot for me as it is better performing and much better value. I just think it is better. Buying it now will give it five or six weeks or so to maturate properly and it'll be really good by the time the summer comes around in earnest here. There is a lot to be said for having something to look forward to and I am very much looking forward to wearing this on a night out or two here in the summer.

Hopefully it will not just be the weather that is better here by then. Here's hoping.

Three Word Summary - Lime Coconut Memories

Rayhaan Aquatica Review/Compared to VIW
For the price, this fragrance is extremely comparable to the original Creed VIW. Opens with a bright, fresh cut lime note that is very addictive in the air. If you have ever had a limon paleta, it is that exact creamy but bright lime opening. The mid starts to indulge more into the coconut within the fragrance, it is a more coconut milk vibe, adding a little bit of creaminess to the fragrance, not in a lactonic way, but thickening up the bright lime into the drydown. The drydown is a lime-forward coconut milk, and I really love it personally. It doesn't go into that coconut sunscreen note that some people associate with coconut; it is surprisingly natural. I get 5-6 hours of longevity from my bottle, not amazing, but still an improvement from the original Creed fragrance. Doing a direct side-by-side, Aquatica has more lime to my nose, while overall being a more projecting fragrance. If someone smells this in the air, they're going to immediately know that it is Creed VIW. If they're familiar with the fragrance, it really is so close, maybe a 9/10 clone?

What a stunner
I personally love VIW by Creed. It’s a special fragrance that is just so easy to love and one that I find myself sniffing everytime I approach my fragrance collection. There’s something so calming and alluring to it. However, it is well documented what its major pitfall is- longevity. For all its stunning scent qualities, it falls short of any inkling of performance. It’s gone within 2 hrs, like it wasn’t even there. It also really doesn’t project much. It doesn’t fill a room either.

Enter Rayhaan Aquatica. I have waited for you for a long time. There were a few other attempts by different companies to get close (Malibu party in the bay, st barts, Hawaii volcano, etc) but none were as accessible nor performed as well. And if they did they were not really close to VIW and more of an inspired by type of frag. Also Malibu party in the bay is crazy pricey for a dupe and good luck finding it for sale.

Aquatica is about 97-99% there. It’s a little stronger on the lime note (some say it’s closer to the OG VIW, which I have never sniffed). But all the other parts of VIW are there. The coconut, white florals, and clean musk all find their way into the Rayhaan take and develop over time. Speaking of time, this thing lasts. It pushes for a good 2 hrs and then settles close to the surface. I sprayed my chest before bed and was pleasantly surprised to smell it in the morning. Without having to stick my nose directly into my shirt. So you’ll be able to detect it for at least 8 hrs on clothes. On skin a little less. Full disclosure, my skin is a bit dry due to cold weather, heater running, and my water softener system not working atm.

For the price this is an easy scoop. I got mine from BeautyHouse on TikTok. It’s less than $40. If you like that VIW scent or even enjoy a limey coconut scent this is very easy to like. And its performance is great. It’s not nuclear nor room filling. It’s just right. This bottle has incredible value at its price point. This is like Liquid Brun caliber. I might end up selling my bottle of VIW as it is now just taking up space on my shelf and have a much better performer on my hands.

Not for me
I like Creed VIW and have been considering it for a while. This scent came up that a lot of people recommend as getting close to the VIW DNA, so I was intrigued.

I blind bought a bottle and I was quite excited to try it out hoping this would be like a CDNIM-type of hit.

The opening I was hit was a blast of lemon and as it dried down I was getting hits of coconut mixed in. It's an okay scent in the air but up close it smells like cleaning detergent with coconut. I wore it a couple times to the gym and i could smell a lime/coconut bubble around me the whole time.

It's just not for me though at all. It's something I might keep for the occasional beach day as in the air it smells very playful but not something I'll reach for very often.

It's similar to VIW in the notes but VIW is just a much better scent - there's no comparison. This smells juvenile in comparison to VIW. For £30 though can't complain too much. It performs much better than VIW.

Lime Juice
I have heard that some people have differences in scent based on batches and I do not have any great detail to share on that front. Mine, even after months of having it (so yes, it has "matured"- not that it was intentional, I just bought it and time kept moving regardless) smells like I am spraying myself with lime juice.

Projects like crazy for about 20 mins, then the sillage calms a bit. Upon dry down I get hints of cream. No coconut or rum. However, that isn't to say it's bad- but I am not sure where some people get all this crazy complexity. Perhaps they are being paid to overhype it. It's an awesome, limey summer fragrance.
