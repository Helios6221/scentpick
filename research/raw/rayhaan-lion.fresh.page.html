GRAN PERFUME
Si bien es un perfume mas sintético que el que intenta representar (UltraMale de JPG), en sus similitudes diría que esta mas inclinado a una salida bien limpia y dulce, cargando la canela, la vainilla el ambar y una base cítrica bien integrada en la ecuación olfativa, luego se asoma la frescura de la menta. Ya en desarrollo y secado, esa menta junto con los demás aromas descritos se va acentuando con lo dulce de la pera, con lo terroso del pachulí que añade algo más de lo masculino de la fragancia, finalmente el cedro se hace notar en la despedida del perfume que tiene en piel una duración no menor de 9 a 10 horas y en ropa mas de 18 horas, por lo que hay que aplicarlo con mesura.

Me gusta más que la versión de JPG, pues si bien huele algo mas sintético, Rayhaan Lion a mi olfato mejora la idea central de este perfume aromático especiado siendo muchísimo mas bueno en proyección y duración, con un costo muy conveniente. Habiendo probado la versión de 9 PM, definitivamente esta ecuación de Lion representa con mas vigor, dulzura y frescura que la de Afnan en relación a lo que están versionando.

Better than 9PM and Ramz Silver
I bought this fragrance in April, and it's been one of my favorites since then. This is such a very well replicated Ultra Male smell. I have Ultra Male and i've had 9PM and Ramz Silver too, but this one? Side by side it's a nearly identical smell, and it has a pretty damn good chance of being longer lasting than the current ultra male batches. The pear in this fragrance is very well made and it's what separates this from the other clones that lack this note.
The longevity? 9-10 Hours easily on skin with 3-4 hours of crazy projection
The smell might be pretty basic since everyone knows Ultra Male but still, this is a really really good and the closest dupe of Ultra Male.
I recommend this to anyone who likes the DNA and had/wants to buy a fragrance that smells like Ultra Male because this won't dissapoint you.

A Beast - a Lion!
The original fragrance DNA is captured very well. The pear note is also very prominent here. The scent starts intensely sweet, and the lavender is immediately recognizable. The well-known pear note joins in. Very beautifully and high-quality made.

The fragrance has real power. On my skin, it lasts easily 14 hours, with good presence.
Addendum: Even after 24 hours, the scent is still close to the skin!!!

The bottle is also very high-quality. Magnetic cap and pressurized atomizer. Many other fragrance houses could take a cue from this!

All in all, a clear recommendation that makes the original redundant.
