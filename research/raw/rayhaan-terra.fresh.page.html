Rayhaan Terra and the modern perfume trend terror
I always associate this perfume with a big a-ha moment that I've had.
It's not like Outlands has already been out for more than half a year - maybe even longer than that - until a hype around it came about. Quite unusual, wouldn't you think? A ridiculously priced perfume produced by a brand that screams 'opulence' and 'luxury'. But a brand that is hard for people to get their hands on, due to high pricing and scent profiles, that don't necessarily scream "easy wearing".
Regardless, what felt like maybe 2 weeks passed and the hype from influencers was there and OH! would you look at that? A dupe of Outlands has emerged shortly after! What a crazy coincidence, right?
I do believe that Rayhaan need to change their advertising strategies, because there has been a similar wave of content emerging with Tonquin Giza and the original and also VERY expensive Guerlain perfume. See for yourselves soon!

Without that wave of PR I don't think that the score would be this high. And what really gets me is that this scent.. just doesn't need to exist.
I already am of the opinion that Outlands is nothing but a "timid Interlude Man ". Cloning a scent, that justifies its price with some ridiculous gimmicks already is a weird situation to start with.
And the profile of Terra can be found in much more fleshed out perfumes, instead! Boss Bottled Absolu is most of Terra 's elements, but with much more confidence.
Rayhann has good perfumes, that have their time and place. But Terra is not one of them.
Ask yourself this: does the influencer really tell you the truth about closeness in scent profile? Where did they get their bottle?
And most importantly: Do you really need to try a perfume that claims to replicate a scent profile of a 400+$ fragrance that you most likely haven't smelled yet and never will?
Make sure to sample first.
Be conscious of what you're consuming and don't fall into the pit of overconsumption.

Layton’s Drydown + Extra Spice
First let me start off by saying, this stuff smells really good and I really like it. Second, this literally smells like some kind of flanker of Layton. If you take Layton, tone down the aromatics and that cooling effect from the Geranium, switch out the apple with citrus, add some more spices, you get this. I literally just got my bottle and smelt it and I was like “holy shit, that’s Layton”. If you like the deep vanilla of Layton you will undoubtedly like this. I haven’t smelt outlands, so I don’t know if the same applies to that, but I can tell you the opening of this is the drydown of Layton and it’s damn good stuff. As I said, I just got my bottle so I can’t comment on performance or even the drydown yet, but this surprised me so much that I had to jump on and write a review for it straight away. If you’re a fan of sweet but not gourmand vanilla and don’t mind a bit of spice in your frags, this is a must try. I like this at least as much as I like Layton, maybe even more, but this is a touch darker and heavier so less versatile, I think you can get away with wearing Layton in all seasons despite it being on the sweeter side, but I would reserve this for cool spring evenings and winter time.

Outlands but without the opening spice mess
Rayhaan Terra genuinely surprised me. It smells fantastic, and to my nose it's the closest thing to Amouage Outlands (the spice bomb) without the price tag. I have an Outlands sample, and while Outlands starts with an intense, overcrowded spicy blast, Terra opens smoother and more balanced. But the dry down is almost identical. I also find myself reaching for this whenever I'm bored, the scent profile is so interesting that it actually lifts my mood and keeps me sniffing my wrist and easily lasting around 7-8 hours on my skin.

I love this perfume, especially because Outlands is out of my budget. If you've tried Outlands and liked it, you can blind buy this with confidence. If you love the vibe but can't afford Amouage prices, Rayhaan Terra is ABSOLUTELY WORTH IT!!!
