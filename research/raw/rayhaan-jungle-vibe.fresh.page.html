Helpful Review 3
green, green & more green
This is *VERY* green. Just like pistachio, cherry, milk & banana are having a huge moment in perfumery right now. Just like the aforementioned, fig is also trending, and each have varying interpretations on each fruit. The fig in “Jungle Vibe” is herbal, vegetal and green. Fans of Diptique’s “Philophokos” would likely enjoy this.

It’s also ideal for humid, hot weather days as it’s both too light and too misaligned for the cold air and barren trees; this level of verdant greenery would also be out of place during date night or snowy days. “Jungle Vibe” would shine on a masculine person at a brunch, family function or anniversary party.

This is enjoyable but doesn’t blow my mind. If I don’t forget about it completely, I’d recommend to someone who wanted an easy, freshie for summer on the cheap.

Very helpful Review 9
Green Fig for Well-Being
Before I ordered "Jungle Vibe | Rayhaan," I already had it on my radar. Fig and grapefruit are two of my absolute favorite notes; I imagined it somewhere along the lines of Philosykos Eau de Parfum plus more citrus.

In the city center, I was able to smell Santal Greenery for the first time and was immediately convinced. It comes very close to its model, which must simply be unjustifiably expensive, but has more kick in the opening due to the prominent bergamot.

In direct comparison, Santal Greenery is softer and left me with a bit of aloe vera skin cream vibes, which make the model seem slightly feminine. Additionally, the longevity of the over €300 fragrance was relatively poor.

Nevertheless, I had the Dries Van Noten tested alongside other fragrances on one hand and couldn't stop smelling it. So I was able to order Jungle Vibe cheaply on TikTok with coupons.

It opens exactly as I imagined. Citrusy, slightly bitter from the grapefruit, but with a decent portion of green fig. The fig is not as realistic and sweet as in the well-known "Philosykos (Eau de Parfum) | Diptyque," but greener, a bit spicier, and in my opinion, more mass-appealing.

Jungle Vibe comes across clean. Fresh, green, not too sweet. Slightly metallic in the opening, but after 20 minutes, it becomes rounded and clean, slightly soapy and woody.

Overall, a very successful fragrance; I don't understand the bad reviews.
A super mass-appealing flanker of the well-known line with the great bottles, perfect for anyone looking for something unobtrusive and who doesn't want to smell like "0815-Blue-Freshie-Shower Gel." Spicy, clean, green, great fig, summer vibes. Definitely not reminiscent of leaves or grass. Office, gym, everyday signature scent - all possible.

Good that it doesn't resemble Le Beau Paradise Garden much for me, because JPG fragrances are a disgusting synthetic designer mix that I steer clear of, as I don't want headaches.

I really like it and it can be bought blind if you're looking for a different spring/summer fragrance.
