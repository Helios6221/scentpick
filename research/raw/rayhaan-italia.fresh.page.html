A solid Naxos alternative with minor weaknesses
In search of the perfect Naxos dupe, I have now come across "Italia | Rayhaan."
At first, the name made me think of a clone of "Italica | Casamorati," but here the Naxos DNA is unmistakably captured.

The opening starts citrus-fresh with a distinct lavender note. In fact, this resembles that of Xerjoff, but of course, it does not quite reach the quality of the original.
I can also sense the first hints of honey.

In the drydown, the honey note comes fully into play.
While it is not as refined and high-quality as in the original, it still captures the Naxos vibe very well.
The listed tobacco is nowhere to be found here; instead, the tonka bean takes center stage and gives the fragrance a pleasantly vanilla-like quality.
I also perceive the cinnamon subtly; I really like this restrained dosage.

In the end, honey, lavender, tonka bean, and a gentle cinnamon note remain, underscored by a still slightly fresh bergamot in the background.
The fragrance becomes a bit powdery and sweeter towards the end.
Overall, it comes about 85% close to its predecessor.
However, Italia ultimately lacks the depth and quality to completely replace the original.

The longevity is a solid 7 hours, which can vary depending on skin type.
I find the projection to be rather moderate; here, Xerjoff is definitely more potent.
Although Italia is labeled as a unisex fragrance, I am sure that men would wear it more (M65%/35%W).

Conclusion
Definitely one of the better Naxos dupes on the market.
Even though I personally will pass, fans of this DNA can buy it without hesitation, provided they can accept that it is not a perfect 1:1 clone.

Best Naxos Clone?
The similarity to Naxos is clearly recognizable, but overall this scent doesn’t blow me away.

For me, Italia feels like a simplified and cheaper interpretation of the Naxos DNA. The sweetness reminds me more of a cheap Le Male clone that I bought at a Turkish fair back in the day.

The longevity also didn’t really convince me. Especially in comparison to Ateeq by Nusuk, Italia performs significantly worse for me. Ateeq not only offers better performance but also conveys the honey character much more beautifully and authentically.

I would even rank Mystic White (Caline) just ahead of Italia. Italia is certainly a nice fragrance, and those looking for an affordable Naxos alternative might find it here. However, the spark never really ignited for me.

My conclusion: If you like Naxos, you should either go for the original or consider alternatives like Ateeq or Mystic White.
